Private by construction, not by promise

A health log is about as personal as data gets. This one is built so that there is very little to misuse in the first place.

  • No third-party requests

    The signed-in app loads nothing from anyone else. Fonts are self-hosted; there is no ad network and no tracking script anywhere near your log.

  • Your data, your account

    Everything lives in your own record, readable only by you. Export the whole lot to JSON whenever you want — and delete it just as easily.

  • Nothing is sold

    There is no business model that depends on your weight. No profiling, no data brokers, no "anonymised" resale.

Questions worth asking

What does it cost?

Nothing. There is no paid tier and no upsell waiting three screens in.

Can I sign up?

Not at the moment. Vitals runs as a private tracker on an invite-only allowlist, so sign-in only works for permitted accounts — that limit is enforced on the server, not just hidden in the interface.

Do I need an account?

For syncing across devices, yes — that is what ties your data to you and nobody else. It also runs on one device only, where everything stays in that browser and never leaves it.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, as JSON, in one click from Settings. It is your log; a tracker you cannot leave is a trap.

Is it a medical device?

No. Vitals records what you tell it and draws charts. It does not diagnose, treat, or give medical advice — talk to a clinician for that.

Does it work offline?

The app runs in the browser and keeps working on a flaky gym connection; changes sync when you are back on a real one.
