Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026

Vitals is a personal health tracker. The whole point of it is to hold information that is nobody else's business, so this policy is short and specific rather than long and evasive.

The short version. Your health log is stored in your own account, readable only by you. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone. You can export all of it, or delete all of it, whenever you want.

What is stored

Only what you type in, plus the minimum needed to sign you in:

  • Health entries you create — weight and body measurements, meals and their calories and macros, workouts, steps, sleep, mood, water, habit ticks, and your training progress and per-set weights.
  • Your settings — display name, units, theme, and daily targets.
  • Account identifiers from sign-in — the email address and account ID supplied by Google when you sign in, used to tie your record to you and to check you are permitted to sign in at all.

There is no tracking of how you use the app, no session recording, no device fingerprinting, and no advertising identifier.

Where it is stored

Signed in, your record lives in Google Cloud Firestore, and sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication — both operated by Google as the hosting provider. Access is restricted by server-side security rules to the single account that owns the record; that restriction is enforced by the server on every read and write, not merely hidden in the interface.

Running on one device only, everything stays in that browser's local storage and is never transmitted anywhere.

Cookies and analytics

The signed-in app sets no advertising or analytics cookies and makes no third-party requests. Fonts are served from this domain rather than a font CDN. Sign-in requires Google's authentication services, which set the cookies necessary to keep you signed in — those are strictly necessary for a feature you asked for, not tracking.

This public marketing page may use a cookieless analytics counter that records aggregate page views. It stores nothing on your device, sets no cookies, assigns no identifier, and cannot follow you across sites — which is why you are not being asked to dismiss a consent banner. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are honoured, and no analytics runs on the signed-in app at all.

What is never done

  • Your data is not sold, rented, or licensed to anyone.
  • It is not used to train anything or build a profile of you.
  • It is not shared with advertisers, data brokers, insurers, or employers.
  • It is not read for any purpose other than showing it back to you.

Your control over it

  • Export. Settings has a one-click export of your entire record as JSON — everything, in a format you can read and keep.
  • Deletion. Ask and your record is deleted in full. Local-only data is removed by clearing the site data in your browser.
  • Correction. Every entry is editable in the app at any time.

Depending on where you live you may have further statutory rights — access, rectification, erasure, portability, and objection among them. Since this is a single-user private tracker, the practical answer to all of them is the same: it is your record, and you already hold the controls.

Retention

Entries are kept until you delete them, because a health log is only useful as a history. Nothing is auto-expired behind your back.

Children

Vitals is not directed at children under 13 and no account is knowingly created for one.

Security

Traffic is served over HTTPS. The app enforces a strict Content Security Policy, does not load third-party scripts, and ships no source maps of its own code. Access to stored data is enforced by server-side rules. No system is perfectly secure, and no promise to the contrary is made here.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes with it. There is no version of this that quietly starts selling your data.
